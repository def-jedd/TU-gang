| ID | Student question | English tutor response |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Why do objects fall? | Earth's gravity pulls objects downward. When you let go of a ball, it falls toward the ground. |
| 02 | How do plants make food? | Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugar for energy and growth. Think of a leaf as a tiny food-making place. |
| 03 | Why does ice melt? | Ice melts when it takes in heat from its surroundings. An ice cube left on a table slowly becomes liquid water. |
| 04 | Where does a puddle go? | Heat turns some liquid water into water vapor that mixes with the air. That is why a puddle can disappear even when nobody wipes it up. |
| 05 | Why does it rain? | Water vapor cools and forms tiny drops in clouds. When enough drops come together and become heavy, they fall as rain. |
| 06 | Why do earthquakes happen? | Large pieces of Earth's outer layer can suddenly slip along a fault. That movement sends energy through the ground and makes it shake. |
| 07 | Why do volcanoes erupt? | Hot melted rock and gas can build pressure beneath the ground. When they escape through a volcano, an eruption happens. |
| 08 | Why does a rolling ball stop? | Friction between the ball and the ground slows it down. A ball usually rolls farther on a smooth floor than on rough grass. |
| 09 | What is a force? | A force is a push or a pull that can change how something moves. Pushing a chair across the room is one example. |
| 10 | What is energy? | Energy is what allows things to move, heat up, or change. Your body gets energy from food when you run or play. |
| 11 | What does one-half mean? | One-half is one of two equal parts of a whole. If you divide one banana equally between two people, each gets one-half. |
| 12 | Why are two-fourths and one-half equal? | Two of four equal pieces make the same amount as one of two equal pieces. Imagine cutting the same sandwich into two pieces, then cutting each piece in half again. |
| 13 | What does multiplication mean? | Multiplication can count equal groups quickly. Three bags with two oranges in each bag contain six oranges altogether. |
| 14 | What does division mean? | Division can mean sharing equally. If six pencils are shared among three students, each student gets two pencils. |
| 15 | What is perimeter? | Perimeter is the distance around the edge of a shape. You could measure the perimeter of a garden by walking along its boundary. |
| 16 | What is area? | Area tells us how much surface a shape covers. Counting the square tiles that cover a floor is one way to find its area. |
| 17 | What is cause and effect? | A cause is why something happens, and an effect is what happens afterward. Heavy rain can be a cause of a flooded path. |
| 18 | What is a habitat? | A habitat is the place where a living thing finds what it needs to survive. A pond is a habitat for many fish and insects. |
| 19 | Why should we wash our hands with soap? | Soap and water help remove germs and dirt from our hands. Washing before eating lowers the chance of carrying those germs into our food. |
| 20 | Why does a shadow appear? | A shadow forms when an object blocks light. Your body can make a shadow on the ground when sunlight shines behind you. |
