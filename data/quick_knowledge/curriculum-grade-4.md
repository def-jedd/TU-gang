# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 4

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 1

  Competency: Draw and state the properties of triangles and quadrilaterals.
  Starter question: What are the parts of triangles and four-sided shapes?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g4-mathematics-w3-1: Kinds of Triangles**
  Competency: Classify triangles and quadrilaterals according to sides and angles.
  Starter question: How do we group triangles by their sides and angles?
- **g4-mathematics-w3-2: Kinds of Quadrilaterals**
  Competency: Differentiate different quadrilaterals.
  Starter question: How is a square different from other four-sided shapes?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 4

- **g4-mathematics-w4-1: Perimeter**
  Competency: Find the perimeter of quadrilaterals that are not squares or rectangles.
  Starter question: How do I find the distance around a shape?
- **g4-mathematics-w4-2: Perimeter of Combined Shapes**
  Competency: Find the perimeter of composite figures composed of triangles and quadrilaterals.
  Starter question: How do I find the perimeter of a shape made of other shapes?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 5

- **g4-mathematics-w5-1: Numbers to a Million**
  Competency: Read and write numbers up to 1 000 000 in numerals and in words.
  Starter question: How do I read and write numbers up to one million?
- **g4-mathematics-w5-2: Place Value**
  Competency: Determine:
  a. the place value of a digit in a 6-digit number
  b. the value of a digit
  c. the digit of number, given its place value
  Starter question: What is the value of each digit in a big number?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g4-mathematics-w6-1: Comparing Big Numbers**
  Competency: Compare numbers up to 1 000 000 using =, < and >.
  Starter question: How do I compare numbers up to one million?
- **g4-mathematics-w6-2: Rounding Big Numbers**
  Competency: Round numbers to the nearest hundred thousand.
  Starter question: How do I round to the nearest hundred thousand?
- **g4-mathematics-w6-3: Estimating Sums**
  Competency: Estimate the sum and difference of two 5- to 6-digit numbers by rounding the addends to the nearest large place value of the numbers.
  Starter question: How can I quickly guess the answer when adding big numbers?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 7

- **g4-mathematics-w7-1: Adding and Subtracting**
  Competency: Add numbers with sums up to 1 000 000 and subtracts numbers where both numbers are less than 1 000 000, with and without regrouping.
  Starter question: How do I add and subtract big numbers?
- **g4-mathematics-w7-2: Multiplying**
  Competency: Multiply two numbers with and without regrouping:
  a. 3- to 4-digit numbers by a 1-digit number
  b. 2- to 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers, with products up to 1 000 000."
  Starter question: How do I multiply two numbers?
- **g4-mathematics-w7-3: Estimating Products**
  Competency: Estimate the result of multiplying two numbers where the product is less than 1 000 000.
  Starter question: How can I guess the answer to a multiplication?
### Grade 4 Mathematics, Week 9

- **g4-mathematics-w9-1: Multi-Step Problems**
  Competency: Solve multi-step problems involving one or more of the four operations with results of calculations up to 1 000 000, including problems involving money.
  Starter question: How do I solve a problem that needs more than one step?
- **g4-mathematics-w9-2: Dividing**
  Competency: Divide two numbers with and without regrouping:

## Grade 4 Science

### Grade 4 Science, Week 1

- **g4-science-w1-1: Scientists and Inventions**
  Competency: Use information from secondary sources to identify a famous Filipino and/or foreign scientist and their invention/s.
  Starter question: Who are famous scientists and what did they invent?
- **g4-science-w1-2: Inventions at Home**
  Competency: Use information from the home or the local community to identify a science invention and explain its impact on their everyday life.
  Starter question: How do inventions make our daily life easier?
### Grade 4 Science, Week 3

- **g4-science-w3-1: Chemical Properties**
  Competency: Describe the chemical properties of materials, such as they can be burnt, react with other materials, or are degradable or biodegradable.
  Starter question: What happens when materials burn or rot?
- **g4-science-w3-2: Changes When Heated**
  Competency: Describe changes in properties of materials when exposed to certain changes in temperature, such as changes when wood or coal are burned.
  Starter question: What happens to wood or coal when it is burned?
- **g4-science-w3-3: Preventing Harmful Changes**
  Competency: Demonstrate ways to minimize harmful changes in materials, such as restriction of burning of waste materials, and care in handling reactive materials.
  Starter question: How can we prevent harmful changes like burning trash?
### Grade 4 Science, Week 6

- **g4-science-w6-1: Science and Our Community**
  Competency: Identify issues and concerns in the local community and how they could be addressed by science, such as the treatment of waste.
  Starter question: How can science help solve problems like waste?
- **g4-science-w6-2: Doing a Survey**
  Competency: Apply science process skills and attitudes in conducting a guided survey about environmental issues and concerns including grouping and classifying, communicating, and open mindedness.
  Starter question: How do scientists gather information with a survey?
### Grade 4 Science, Week 8

- **g4-science-w8-1: Human Body Systems**
  Competency: Describe in simple terms how the following human body systems work: muscular, skeletal, digestive, circulatory, and respiratory.
  Starter question: How do our bones, muscles, and heart work?
- **g4-science-w8-2: Roots and Shoots**
  Competency: Observe the root and shoot system in plants and describe why they are important.
  Starter question: Why are roots and shoots important to plants?
### Grade 4 Science, Week 10

- **g4-science-w10-1: Habitats**
  Competency: Use a drawing or diagram to classify some Philippine animals and plants, based on their habitat: some live on land (terrestrial), live in water (aquatic) or fly in the air (aerial).
  Starter question: Which animals live on land, in water, or in the air?
- **g4-science-w10-2: Plants and Animals in a Habitat**
  Competency: Make a list or draw up a table with examples of animals and plants in a particular habitat, such as a garden, rice field, seashore, and mangrove swamp.
  Starter question: What plants and animals live in a rice field or a mangrove?
