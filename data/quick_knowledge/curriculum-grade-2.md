# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 2

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g2-gmrc_values-w1-1: Valuing Myself**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagpapahalaga sa sarili sa pamamagitan ng paggamit ng batayang impormasyon sa mga angkop na situwasyon a. Natutukoy ang mga batayang impormasyon tungkol sa sariling pamilya b. Napatutunayan na ang mga batayang impormasyon tungkol sa sariling pamilya ay mahalaga sa pagkakaroon ng kamalayan sa sariling pagkakakilanlan c. Naipahahayag ang mga batayang impormasyon tungkol sa sariling pamilya (hal. pagguhit ng family portrait at pagkuwento tungkol sa pamilya)
  Starter question: How can I show that I value myself?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 3

- **g2-gmrc_values-w3-1: Healthy Habits**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagpapahalaga sa sarili sa pamamagitan ng pagtupad sa mga alituntuning pangkalusugan at pangkaligtasan a. Natutukoy ang mga paraan ng pangangalaga sa kalusugan na natutuhan sa pamilya b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pangangalaga sa kalusugan gabay ang pamilya ay mahalaga sa pagpapanatili ng malusog na isip at katawan c. Naisasakilos ang wastong pangangalaga sa kalusugan na gabay ang pamilya
  Starter question: Why should I follow health rules every day?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 4

- **g2-gmrc_values-w4-1: Being Truthful**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging totoo sa pamamagitan ng wastong pagpapahayag ng tunay na damdamin sa mga pangyayari o situwasyon a. Naiisa-isa ang iba't ibang damdamin b. Naiuugnay ang pag-unawa sa sariling damdamin sa paglinang ng pansariling disiplina na makikita sa kaniyang mga kilos at pakikipag-ugnayan c. Nailalapat ang mga wastong pagtugon sa iba’t ibang nararanasang damdamin
  Starter question: How can I honestly say what I feel?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 6

- **g2-gmrc_values-w6-1: Sharing a Prayer**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging madasalin sa pamamagitan ng pagbabahagi ng sariling panalangin a. Nakapagbibigay ng mga mabuting dulot ng pansariling panalangin b. Napatutunayan na ang pansariling panalangin ay paraan ng pakikipag-ugnayan upang maipahayag ang pagkilala, pasasalamat, at pagmamahal sa Diyos c. Nakabubuo ng sariling panalangin para sa ikabubuti ng sarili at kapuwa
  Starter question: How can I share my own prayer with others?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 7

- **g2-gmrc_values-w7-1: Not Wasting Things**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging masinop sa pamamagitan ng pagtitiyak na walang naaaksaya at nasasayang na gamit o mga bagay sa kapaligiran na maaaring mapakinabangan a. Nakakikilala ng mga wastong paraan ng pagtitipid na nakabubuti sa kapaligiran b. Napatutunayan na ang pagtitipid na nakabubuti sa kapaligiran ay pagganap sa tungkuling gamitin nang wasto at sulitin ang mga bagay c. Naisasakilos ang mga wastong paraan ng pagtitipid na nakabubuti sa kapaligiran
  Starter question: Why should I take care not to waste things?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 8

- **g2-gmrc_values-w8-1: Following Rules**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging masunurin sa pamamagitan ng pagsunod sa iba’t ibang alituntunin sa pamayanan a. Natutukoy ang mga tungkulin ng batang Pilipino sa pamayanan b. Napatutunayan na ang mga sariling tungkulin ng batang Pilipino sa pamayanan ay makatutulong upang hubugin ang pagmamahal sa kaniyang bayan c. Nailalapat ang mga tungkulin sa pamayanan ng batang Pilipino sa iba’t ibang situwasyon
  Starter question: Why should I follow the rules in my community?
### Grade 2 GMRC / Values Education, Week 9

- **g2-gmrc_values-w9-1: Showing My Talents**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay ng tiwala sa sarili sa pamamagitan ng pagpapakita ng kakayahan o talento a. Natutukoy ang sariling kakayahan o talento sa tulong ng pamilya b. Napatutunayan na ang pagkilala ng sariling kakayahan o talento sa tulong ng pamilya ay naglilinang sa kaniyang taglay na kalakasan tungo sa pagkakaroon ng tiwala sa sarili c. Nakapagbabahagi ng sariling kakayahan o talento na natuklasan sa tulong ng pamilya
  Starter question: How can I feel confident showing my talents?

## Grade 2 Mathematics

### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 1

- **g2-mathematics-w1-1: Circles**
  Competency: Represent and describe circles, half circles and quarter circles.
  Starter question: What are circles, half circles, and quarter circles?
- **g2-mathematics-w1-2: Combining Shapes**
  Competency: Compose and decompose composite figures made up of squares, rectangles, triangles, circles, half circles, and quarter circles, using cut-outs and square grids.
  Starter question: How can I make a big shape from smaller shapes?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 2

- **g2-mathematics-w2-1: Sliding Shapes**
  Competency: Describe and draw the effect of a one-direction multi-step slide (or translation) in basic shapes and figures.
  Starter question: What happens to a shape when I slide it?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g2-mathematics-w3-1: Counting to 1000**
  Competency: Count up to 1000.
  Starter question: How do I count up to 1000?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 4

- **g2-mathematics-w4-1: Writing Numbers**
  Competency: Read and write numerals up to 1000.
  Starter question: How do I read and write numbers up to 1000?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 5

- **g2-mathematics-w5-1: Showing Big Numbers**
  Competency: Recognize and represent numbers up to 1000 using a variety of concrete and pictorial models, and numerals.
  Starter question: How can I show a number up to 1000 using blocks?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g2-mathematics-w6-1: Skip Counting**
  Competency: Count by 2s, 5s, 10s, 20s, 50s, and 100s (not beyond 1000).
  Starter question: How do I count by 2s, 5s, and 10s?
- **g2-mathematics-w6-2: Ordering Numbers**
  Competency: Order numbers up to 1000 from smallest to largest, and vice versa.
  Starter question: How do I put numbers in order up to 1000?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 7

- **g2-mathematics-w7-1: Ordinal Numbers**
  Competency: Describe the position of objects using ordinal numbers up to 20th.
  Starter question: What do words like fifth and twentieth mean?
- **g2-mathematics-w7-2: Place Value**
  Competency: Determine:
  a. the place value of a digit in a 3-digit number
  b. the value of a digit
  c. the digit of a number, given its place value
  Starter question: What is the value of each digit in a number?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 8

- **g2-mathematics-w8-1: Adding on a Number Line**
  Competency: Illustrate addition of 2-digit and by 1-digit numbers as “counting up” on the number line.
  Starter question: How can I add by counting up on a number line?
- **g2-mathematics-w8-2: Adding Big Numbers**
  Competency: Add numbers with sums up to 1000 in expanded form.
  Starter question: How do I add numbers up to 1000?
- **g2-mathematics-w8-3: Adding With Regrouping**
  Competency: Add numbers with sums up to 1000, with or without regrouping.
  Starter question: What does it mean to carry when adding?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 9

- **g2-mathematics-w9-1: Rules of Adding**
  Competency: Illustrate and apply the following properties of addition using sums up to 1000:
  a. the sum of zero and any number is equal to the number
  b. changing the order of the addends does not change the sum
  c. changing the grouping of the addends does not change the sum.
  Starter question: Does the order of numbers matter when I add?
- **g2-mathematics-w9-2: Counting Money**
  Competency: Determine and write the value of a number of bills, or a number of coins, or a combination of bills and coins up to ₱1000 (centavo coins only, peso coins only, peso bills only, combined peso coins and peso bills).
  Starter question: How do I count the value of coins and bills?
### Grade 2 Mathematics, Week 10

- **g2-mathematics-w10-1: Comparing Money**
  Competency: Compare the values of different denominations of peso coins and bills up to ₱1000.
  Starter question: Which is worth more, a coin or a bill?
- **g2-mathematics-w10-2: Money Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving addition with sums up to 1000, including problems involving money, with and without regrouping.
  Starter question: How do I solve problems about buying things?
