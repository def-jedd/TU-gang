# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 3

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 3 English

### Grade 3 English, Week 1

- **g3-english-w1-1: Sight Words**
  Competency: Identify Grade 3 level-appropriate sight words.
  Starter question: What are sight words and why should I know them?
- **g3-english-w1-2: Word Patterns**
  Competency: Read words accurately and automatically according to word patterns (initial, final, medial).
  a. CVCC words
  Starter question: How do word patterns help me read faster?
- **g3-english-w1-3: Common Words**
  Competency: Identify high-frequency words accurately.
  Starter question: How can I read common words quickly?
- **g3-english-w1-4: New Words**
  Competency: Use vocabulary referring to:
  a. regional themes
  Starter question: What new words can I use to talk about my region?
- **g3-english-w1-5: Subject Words**
  Competency: Use content-specific words.
  a. Mathematics (basic symbols and terminologies)
  Starter question: What special words do we use in math and science?
- **g3-english-w1-6: Kinds of Words**
  Competency: Identify words with different functions.
  a. words that replace persons, places, things, animals, events, ideas, and emotions
  i. demonstrative pronouns
  Starter question: What jobs do different words do in a sentence?
- **g3-english-w1-7: Reading Words Correctly**
  Competency: Read words correctly for meaning (based on word patterns).
  Starter question: How do word patterns help me read words?
- **g3-english-w1-8: Writing Words**
  Competency: Write words legibly and correctly (based on word patterns).
  Starter question: How can I write words neatly and correctly?
- **g3-english-w1-9: What Is a Sentence?**
  Competency: Identify sentences and non-sentences.
  Starter question: How do I know if a group of words is a sentence?
- **g3-english-w1-10: Simple Sentences**
  Competency: Use simple sentences to express ideas about regional themes.
  Starter question: How do I make a simple sentence about my region?
- **g3-english-w1-11: Reading With Expression**
  Competency: Use simple sentences with proper intonation (pitch, juncture, rhythm).
  Starter question: How should my voice go up and down when I read a sentence?
- **g3-english-w1-12: Word Order**
  Competency: Sequence words to represent meaning in simple sentences.
  a. telling sentences (declarative)
  Starter question: Why does the order of words in a sentence matter?
- **g3-english-w1-13: Parts of a Sentence**
  Competency: Identify the parts of simple sentences: (who/what, what are they doing, when/where/how).
  a. telling sentences (declarative)
  Starter question: What are the parts of a simple sentence?
- **g3-english-w1-14: Capitals and Periods**
  Competency: Use correct capitalization and punctuation for simple sentences. a. telling sentences (declarative)
  Starter question: When do I use a capital letter and a period?
- **g3-english-w1-15: Reading Smoothly**
  Competency: Read grade level sentences with appropriate speed, accuracy, and expression.
  Starter question: How can I read sentences smoothly and correctly?
- **g3-english-w1-16: Understanding Stories**
  Competency: Comprehend stories.
  a. Note important elements from stories (characters, setting, events).
  b. Sequence at least four to five events.
  c. Identify the problem and solution in stories.
  Starter question: How do I understand a story I read?
- **g3-english-w1-17: Polite Greetings**
  Competency: Use common expressions and polite greetings appropriate to a given situation.
  Starter question: What polite words should I use when I greet people?
- **g3-english-w1-18: Retelling Legends and Fables**
  Competency: Use own words in retelling myths, legends, fables, and narrative poems.
  Starter question: How can I retell a legend or fable in my own words?
- **g3-english-w1-19: Sharing My Experiences**
  Competency: Express ideas about one’s experiences.
  a. regional themes
  Starter question: How can I tell others about something I did?
- **g3-english-w1-20: Using Common Words**
  Competency: Use basic sight words, high frequency, and content-specific words.
  Starter question: How can I use common words when I write?

## Grade 3 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 3 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g3-gmrc_values-w1-1: Growing My Talents**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang tiwala sa sarili sa pamamagitan ng kawilihan na paunlarin ang kaniyang hilig at kakayahan a. Naiisa-isa ang sariling hilig at kakayahan b. Napatutunayan na ang sariling hilig at kakayahan ay mahalaga upang makilala ang sarili bilang bata c. Nailalapat ang sariling hilig at kakayahan tungo sa kakayahan sa pagpapatibay ng tiwala sa sarili
  Starter question: How can I become more confident by growing my talents?
### Grade 3 GMRC / Values Education, Week 2

- **g3-gmrc_values-w2-1: Caring for Others**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging mapagmalasakit sa pamamagitan ng tamang paglalaan ng mga pera o gamit na angkop sa iba’t ibang situwasyon a. Nakakikilala ng mga paraan ng pagpapatatag ng mga gawi sa pag-iimpok at pagtitipid kasama ang pamilya b. Napatutunayan na ang pagpapatatag ng mga gawi sa pag-iimpok at pagtitipid kasama ang pamilya ay magpapaunlad ng sariling kasanayan na mapamahalaan ang higit na mas malaking halaga ng pera o mga gamit sa kinabukasan c. Naisasakilos ang mga paraan ng pagpapatatag ng mga gawi sa pag-iimpok at pagtitipid kasama ang pamilya
  Starter question: How can I share money or things to help others?
### Grade 3 GMRC / Values Education, Week 4

- **g3-gmrc_values-w4-1: Being Responsible**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging responsable sa pamamagitan ng wastong pagtupad sa kaniyang mga gampanin batay sa mga tagubilin ng magulang at nakatatanda a. Natutukoy ang mga gawain ng isang bata sa tahanan at paaralan
  b. Napatutunayan na ang mga sariling gampanin ng bata sa tahanan at paaralan ay nakatutulong upang mahubog ang pagiging responsable sa kanilang gawain at kilos c. Nakapagbabahagi ng mga karanasan sa pagtugon sa mga gampanin ng isang bata sa tahanan at paaralan

Nailalapat ang mga pagkilos na nagpapakita ng paggalang sa Diyos
