# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 9

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 9 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies)

## Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g9-gmrc_values-w1-1: Critical Thinking**
  Competency: Naisasabuhay ang mapanuring pag-iisip sa pamamagitan ng pagsasaalang-alang sa impluwensiya ng pagpapahalaga sa pagpapalalim ng kamalayan sa sarili a. Naiisa-isa ang mga paraan ng pagpapalalim ng kamalayan sa sarili batay sa mga pagpapahalaga b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pagpapalalim ng kamalayan sa sarili batay sa mga pagpapahalaga ay makatutulong upang maisabuhay ang mga katangian at gawi ng lahing pinagmulan na makabubuti sa kaniyang kaganapan bilang tao c. Naisasakilos ang mga paraan ng pagpapalalim ng kamalayan sa sarili batay sa mga pagpapahalaga
  Starter question: How do my values affect the way I think?
### Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education, Week 2

- **g9-gmrc_values-w2-1: Wise Decisions**
  Competency: Naisasabuhay ang matalinong pagpapasiya sa pamamagitan ng paghahanda ng sarili sa mga pangangailangan (requirements) at hamon ng pipiliing karera a. Natutukoy ang mga salik sa pagpili ng karera ayon sa gabay ng pamilya b. Naipaliliwanag na ang mga salik sa pagpili ng karera ay dapat isasaalang-alang upang matiyak ang kahandaan sa pagpapasiya sa nais tahakin na landas sa hinaharap tungo sa matagumpay at makahulugang pamumuhay c. Nakalilikha ng personal na plano sa karera na may pagsasaalang-alang sa mga salik ayon sa gabay ng pamilya
  Starter question: How can I prepare myself to make wise decisions?
### Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education, Week 3

- **g9-gmrc_values-w3-1: Being Responsible**
  Competency: Naisasabuhay ang pagiging responsable sa pamamagitan ng paghahanda sa sarili sa mga posibleng hamon ng binubuong personal na misyon sa buhay a. Nakakikilala ng mga paraan upang mabuo ang personal na misyon sa buhay ayon sa kakayahan b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pagbuo sa personal na misyon sa buhay ay nagbibigay-kahulugan at direksiyon na mamuhay nang makabuluhan upang itaguyod ang kabutihan at kaunlaran ng sarili at lipunan c. Nailalapat ang mga paraan upang mabuo ang personal na misyon sa buhay ayon sa kakayahan
  Starter question: How can I prepare for challenges as a teenager?
### Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education, Week 4

- **g9-gmrc_values-w4-1: Integrity**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay ng integridad sa pamamagitan ng matapat na paggawa ng kaniyang tungkulin sa lahat ng pagkakataon a. Nailalarawan ang paggawa na ginagabayan ng pananampalataya o paniniwala b. Napatutunayan na ang paggawa na may gabay ng pananampalataya o paniniwala ay moral na tungkulin bilang pakikibahagi upang makamit ang kaganapan ng kaniyang pagkatao c. Nailalapat ang paggawa batay sa pananampalataya o paniniwala sa mga nakaatang na gawain sa tahanan at paaralan

### Grade 9 GMRC / Values Education, Week 5

- **g9-gmrc_values-w5-1: Thinking Before Deciding**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa maingat na paghusga sa pamamagitan ng pananahimik, pagninilay, at pananalangin bago magpasiya a. Natutukoy ang papel ng konsensiya sa mapanagutang pagpapasiya na nakaugat sa pananampalataya o paniniwala b. Naipaliliwanag na ang mapanagutang pagpapasiya gabay ang pananampalataya o paniniwala ay ang maingat na paghusga ng isip na piliin ang mabuti at iwasan ang masama sa kongkretong situwasyon c. Nailalapat ang mapanagutang pagpapasiya na nakaugat sa pananampalataya o paniniwala

## Grade 9 Mathematics

### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 1

- **g9-mathematics-w1-1: Points, Lines, and Planes**
  Competency: Illustrate and describe point, line, ray, line segment, angle, and plane using models and geometric notations.
  Starter question: What are points, lines, rays, and planes?
- **g9-mathematics-w1-2: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines**
  Competency: Construct perpendicular and parallel lines.
  Starter question: How do I construct parallel and perpendicular lines?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 2

- **g9-mathematics-w2-1: Transversals**
  Competency: Identify the relationships between angles formed by parallel lines cut by a transversal.
  Starter question: What angles are formed when a line crosses two parallel lines?
- **g9-mathematics-w2-2: Finding Angle Measures**
  Competency: Determine angle measures involving angle pairs, parallel and perpendicular lines, and parallel lines cut by a transversal.
  Starter question: How do I find unknown angles formed by parallel lines?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g9-mathematics-w3-1: Relations and Functions**
  Competency: Identify relations that are functions based on the definitions of relations and functions.
  Starter question: What makes a relation a function?
- **g9-mathematics-w3-2: Domain and Range**
  Competency: Determine the domain and the range of a function expressed in different representations.
  Starter question: What are the domain and range of a function?
- **g9-mathematics-w3-3: Writing Functions**
  Competency: Express the relationship between two variables as a function.
  Starter question: How can I show the relationship between two variables as a function?
- **g9-mathematics-w3-4: Slope**
  Competency: Determine the slopes (as rate of change) and the zeros of linear functions represented in:
  a. graphs
  b. equations
  c. tables of values
  Starter question: What is the slope of a line?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 5

- **g9-mathematics-w5-1: Graphing Linear Functions**
  Competency: Graph a linear function and determine its:
  a. domain
  b. range
  c. intercepts
  d. slope
  Starter question: How do I graph a linear function?
- **g9-mathematics-w5-2: Linear Relationships in Real Life**
  Competency: Represent linear relationships found in real-life situations using different representations.
  Starter question: How can a straight-line graph describe real life?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g9-mathematics-w6-1: Linear Function Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving linear functions.
  Starter question: How do I solve problems with linear functions?
- **g9-mathematics-w6-2: Parallel and Perpendicular Conditions**
  Competency: Determine conditions that guarantee parallelism and perpendicularity of lines.
  Starter question: How can I tell if two lines are parallel or perpendicular?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 7

- **g9-mathematics-w7-1: Classifying Quadrilaterals**
  Competency: Classify quadrilaterals based on formal definitions.
  Starter question: How do we classify four-sided shapes?
- **g9-mathematics-w7-2: Properties of Parallelograms**
  Competency: Use properties of parallelograms to find measures of angles, sides, perpendicular height, and diagonals.
  Starter question: What are the properties of a parallelogram?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 9

- **g9-mathematics-w9-1: Parallelogram Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving parallelograms, rectangles, squares, or rhombuses by applying their different properties.
  Starter question: How do I solve problems about rectangles and rhombuses?
### Grade 9 Mathematics, Week 10

- **g9-mathematics-w10-1: Proving Properties**
  Competency: Prove properties of parallelograms by applying the relevant theorems.
  Starter question: How do we prove the properties of parallelograms?

## Grade 9 Science

### Grade 9 Science, Week 1

- **g9-science-w1-1: Inertia**
  Competency: Identify inertia as the tendency for an object to stay at rest or in motion unless acted on by an unbalanced net force.
  Starter question: Why do we lurch forward when a jeepney suddenly stops?
- **g9-science-w1-2: Acceleration**
  Competency: Demonstrate in practical situations and describe that acceleration is a change in speed and/or direction as the result of a net force.
  Starter question: What is acceleration?
- **g9-science-w1-3: Force, Mass, and Acceleration**
  Competency: Investigate the relationship among force, acceleration, and mass.
  Starter question: How are force, mass, and acceleration related?
- **g9-science-w1-4: Action and Reaction**
  Competency: Explain that when any two objects interact, there are equal but opposite forces exerted between them, which is evident in many practical situations and applications.
  Starter question: Why does a boat move back when I step off it?
- **g9-science-w1-5: Action-Reaction Pairs**
  Competency: Observe and identify action-reaction pairs in everyday situations such as stepping off a boat, or a book on a table, and draw force diagrams to explain how the pairs affect the motion of objects.
  Starter question: Where do I see action and reaction forces every day?
### Grade 9 Science, Week 3

- **g9-science-w3-1: Electricity and Safety**
  Competency: Identify that electricity is a flow of electrons and show appreciation for the need to observe safe measures in handling electricity.
  Starter question: What is electricity and how can I use it safely?
- **g9-science-w3-2: Current, Voltage, and Resistance**
  Competency: Participate in guided investigations to infer the relationship among current, voltage, and resistance in assembled series and parallel circuits with varying number of loads and battery.
  Starter question: How are current, voltage, and resistance related?
- **g9-science-w3-3: Series and Parallel Circuits**
  Competency: Draw diagrams of and assemble series and parallel circuits, showing switch, battery, loads/resistors, ammeter, and voltmeter.
  Starter question: What is the difference between series and parallel circuits?
- **g9-science-w3-4: Choosing a Circuit**
  Competency: Collaborate in a class discussion to recognize the advantages and limitations of using series or parallel circuits.
  Starter question: When is a parallel circuit better than a series circuit?
- **g9-science-w3-5: Electromagnetic Radiation**
  Competency: Describe electromagnetic radiation (EMR) as energy that is created by the vibrations of electrically charged particles which allows it to travel through materials or space as transverse waves.
  Starter question: What is electromagnetic radiation?
- **g9-science-w3-6: Kinds of Electromagnetic Waves**
  Competency: Compare the relative wavelengths and frequencies of different types of electromagnetic waves, including radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultra-violet, x-rays, and gamma radiation.
  Starter question: How are radio waves, light, and X-rays different?
- **g9-science-w3-7: Uses of Electromagnetic Waves**
  Competency: Identify practical applications of electromagnetic radiation, such as radio waves used in telecommunications, and x-rays and gamma rays in medicine.
  Starter question: How do we use radio waves and microwaves?
- **g9-science-w3-8: Dangers of Radiation**
  Competency: Gather information from secondary sources to explain the harmful effects that EMR can have on living things.
  Starter question: How can some radiation harm living things?
### Grade 9 Science, Week 9

- **g9-science-w9-1: Continental Drift**
  Competency: Identify and explain evidence that current continents are separate parts of what was a single continent millions of years ago.
  Starter question: Were the continents once joined together?
- **g9-science-w9-2: Earth's Plates**
  Competency: Participate in a collaborative group or class task to examine and describe the topographical and geological evidence for plate boundaries occurring in the area where the Philippines is located.
  Starter question: How do mountains and trenches form?
- **g9-science-w9-3: Plate Boundaries**
  Competency: Describe the types of plate boundaries found around the Earth.
  Starter question: What happens where Earth's plates meet?
