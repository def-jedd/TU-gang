# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 1

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g1-gmrc_values-w1-1: Self-Confidence**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang tiwala sa sarili sa pamamagitan ng paggamit ng mga batayang impormasyon sa mga angkop na situwasyon a. Nakakikilala ng mga batayang impormasyon ng sarili b. Naiuugnay ang batayang impormasyon ng sarili sa mahalagang bahagi ng pagkilala c. Naipahahayag ang mga batayang impormasyon ng sarili (hal. pangalan, edad, kasarian, magulang, tirahan, petsa ng kapanganakan, palayaw, mga gusto o hilig at paniniwala o relihiyon)
  Starter question: How can I feel proud and sure of myself?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 2

- **g1-gmrc_values-w2-1: Being Truthful**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging totoo sa pamamagitan ng mabuting pakikipag-ugnayan sa kapuwa a. Naiisa-isa ang mga wastong paraan sa pakikipagkaibigan b. Naiuugnay na ang pagkakaroon ng kaibigan sa pagbuo ng ugnayan sa kapuwa na tanggap ang pagkakapareho at pagkakaiba ng mga tao c. Nakapagbabahagi ng mga wastong paraan ng pakikipagkaibigan (hal. paggamit ng magagalang na pananalita, pag-unawa sa pagkakaiba-iba, wastong pagtawag sa pangalan)
  Starter question: Why should I always tell the truth to others?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 3

- **g1-gmrc_values-w3-1: Saving Money**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging matiyaga sa pamamagitan ng palagiang pagtatabi ng mga naipong pera sa alkansiya o mga gamit sa lagayan a. Natutukoy ang mga paraan ng pag-iimpok at pagtitipid ayon sa sariling kakayahan b. Naisasaalang-alang ang sariling paraan ng pag-iimpok at pagtitipid na makatutulong upang matugunan ang kaniyang pangangailangan c. Nailalapat ang mga paraan ng pag-iimpok at pagtitipid (hal. pagtatabi ng pera o gamit sa paaralan)
  Starter question: Why should I save my coins in a piggy bank?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 5

- **g1-gmrc_values-w5-1: Praying**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging madasalin sa pamamagitan ng wastong kilos at salita sa pananalangin a. Natutukoy ang mga sariling paraan ng pananalangin b. Natutuklasan na ang sariling paraan ng pananalangin ay nakatutulong sa pagpapabuti ng ugali c. Nakapagbabahagi ng mga karanasan tungkol sa pagpapabuti ng kaniyang ugali dahil sa pananalangin (hal. disiplina, pagdarasal, nangunguna sa pagdarasal sa klase)
  Starter question: How should I act when I pray?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 6

- **g1-gmrc_values-w6-1: Being Thankful**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging mapagpasalamat sa pamamagitan ng pag-iingat ng mga yamang mula sa kapaligiran
  a. Nakakikilala ng mga paraan ng pag-iingat sa mga yaman mula sa kapaligiran b. Naiuugnay na ang sariling paraan ng pag-iingat sa mga yaman mula sa kapaligiran ay pagpapasalamat sa mga biyayang tinatamasa c. Naipahahayag ang pagpapasalamat sa mga yaman na mula sa kapaligiran
  Starter question: How can I show I am thankful for nature?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 7

- **g1-gmrc_values-w7-1: Respect**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang pagiging magalang sa pamamagitan ng mga angkop na kilos na nagbibigay-halaga sa mga karapatang tinatamasa bilang bata a. Natutukoy ang mga sariling karapatan ng bata (Rights of a Child) (hal. pangalan, edukasyon, pagkain, tubig, tahanan, pamilya) b. Natutuklasan na ang mga sariling karapatan (Rights of a Child) bilang bata ay nagpapabuti sa kaniyang kapakanan c. Naipahahayag ang pagiging magalang sa mga karapatan ng bata na kaniyang natatamasa
  Starter question: How do I show respect to other people?
### Grade 1 GMRC / Values Education, Week 9

- **g1-gmrc_values-w9-1: Keeping Clean**
  Competency: Naipakikita ang kalinisan sa pamamagitan ng palagiang pagsunod sa mga alituntunin sa paglilinis ng katawan ayon sa gabay ng pamilya, tagapangalaga, o nakatatanda a. Naiisa-isa ang mga paraan ng kalinisan sa katawan na natutuhan b. Natutuklasan na ang kalinisan sa katawan ayon sa gabay ng pamilya ay may mabuting epekto sa sariling kalusugan c. Nailalapat ang mga paraan ng kalinisan sa katawan ayon sa gabay ng pamilya, tagapangalaga ,at nakatatatanda (hal. paghuhugas ng kamay bago at pagkatapos kumain, paliligo araw-araw, pagsusuot ng malinis na damit, pagsisipilyo)
  Starter question: Why do I need to keep my body clean?

### Grade 1 Language, Week 1

- **g1-language-w1-1: Talking About Myself**
  Competency: Talk about one’s personal experiences.
  a. oneself and family
  b. content-specific topics
  Starter question: How can I tell others about me and my family?
- **g1-language-w1-2: Drawing My Ideas**
  Competency: Express ideas using a variety of symbols (e.g., drawings, emojis, scribbles).
  a. oneself and family
  b. content-specific topics
  Starter question: How can I share my ideas with drawings?
- **g1-language-w1-3: Family Words**
  Competency: Use high-frequency and content-specific words referring to oneself and family.
  Starter question: What words can I use to talk about my family?
- **g1-language-w1-4: Singing Together**
  Competency: Participate in and contribute to group oral language activities (e.g., singing, chanting, sabayang bigkas).
  Starter question: Why is it fun to sing and chant with my class?
- **g1-language-w1-5: Sounds of My Language**
  Competency: Notice the features (e.g., sounds, intonation, signs) of their first language and other languages in one’s context.
  Starter question: How do the sounds of my language differ from other languages?
- **g1-language-w1-6: Language and Culture**
  Competency: Recognize how language reflects cultural practices and norms.
  a. Share about the language(s) spoken at home
  b. Share words and phrases in their language
  Starter question: How does the way we talk show our culture?
- **g1-language-w1-7: Enjoying Stories**
  Competency: View and listen to a range of texts for enjoyment and interest.
  Starter question: Why is it fun to listen to stories?
- **g1-language-w1-8: Responding to Stories**
  Competency: Engage with or respond to a short spoken texts.
  a. View or listen to spoken texts
  Starter question: What can I say after I hear a story?
- **g1-language-w1-9: Choosing a Book**
  Competency: Give reason/s for choosing books/texts for enjoyment and interest.
  Starter question: How do I choose a book I will enjoy?
- **g1-language-w1-10: Telling What Happened**
  Competency: Record and report ideas and events using some learnt vocabulary.
  a. Relate ideas or events to one’s experience
  Starter question: How can I tell others what happened?
- **g1-language-w1-11: Ideas From Stories and Pictures**
  Competency: Draw and discuss information or ideas from a range of text (e.g., stories, images, digital texts). a. Note and describe main points (e.g., main characters and events) b. Relate ideas or events to one’s experience
  Starter question: What can I learn from stories and pictures?

## Grade 1 Mathematics

### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 1

- **g1-mathematics-w1-1: 2D Shapes**
  Competency: Identify simple 2-dimensional shapes (triangle, rectangle, square) of different size and in different orientation.
  Starter question: What are triangles, rectangles, and squares?
- **g1-mathematics-w1-2: Sides and Corners**
  Competency: Compare and distinguish 2-dimensional shapes according to features such as sides and corners.
  Starter question: How do I tell shapes apart by their sides and corners?
- **g1-mathematics-w1-3: Making Shapes**
  Competency: Compose and decompose triangles, squares, and rectangles.
  Starter question: How can I make new shapes from triangles and squares?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 2

- **g1-mathematics-w2-1: Counting to 100**
  Competency: Count up to 100 (includes counting up or down from a given number and identifying a number that is 1 more or 1 less than a given number).
  Starter question: How do I count up to 100?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g1-mathematics-w3-1: Writing Numbers**
  Competency: Read and write numerals up to 100.
  Starter question: How do I read and write numbers up to 100?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 4

- **g1-mathematics-w4-1: Showing Numbers**
  Competency: Recognize and represent numbers up to 100 using a variety of concrete and pictorial models (e.g., number line, block or bar models, and numerals).
  Starter question: How can I show a number using blocks or pictures?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g1-mathematics-w6-1: Comparing Numbers**
  Competency: Compare two numbers up to 20.
  Starter question: How do I know which number is bigger?
- **g1-mathematics-w6-2: Ordering Numbers**
  Competency: Order numbers up to 20 from smallest to largest, and vice versa.
  Starter question: How do I put numbers in order from smallest to biggest?
- **g1-mathematics-w6-3: First, Second, Third**
  Competency: Describe the position of objects using ordinal numbers: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, up to 10th.
  Starter question: What do first, second, and third mean?
- **g1-mathematics-w6-4: Breaking Numbers Apart**
  Competency: Compose and decompose numbers up to 10 using concrete materials (e.g., 5 is 5 and 0; 4 and 1; 3 and 2; 2 and 3; 1 and 4; 0 and 5).
  Starter question: How can I split a number into two smaller numbers?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 8

- **g1-mathematics-w8-1: Adding Numbers**
  Competency: Illustrate addition of numbers with sums up to 20 using a variety of concrete and pictorial models and describes addition as “counting up,” and “putting together.”
  Starter question: What does it mean to add numbers?
- **g1-mathematics-w8-2: Rules of Adding**
  Competency: Illustrate by applying the following properties of addition, using sums up to 20:
  a. the sum of zero and any number is equal to the number
  a. changing the order of the addends does not change the sum.
  Starter question: Does the order of numbers matter when I add?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 9

- **g1-mathematics-w9-1: Adding in Word Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems (given orally or in pictures) involving addition with sums up to 20.
  Starter question: How do I solve a story problem by adding?
- **g1-mathematics-w9-2: Measuring Length**
  Competency: Measure the length of an object and the distance between two objects using non-standard units.
  Starter question: How can I measure things using my hand or a string?
### Grade 1 Mathematics, Week 10

- **g1-mathematics-w10-1: Comparing Lengths**
  Competency: Compare lengths and distances using non-standard units.
  Starter question: How do I know which thing is longer?
- **g1-mathematics-w10-2: Length Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving lengths and distances using non-standard units.
  Starter question: How do I solve problems about how long things are?
