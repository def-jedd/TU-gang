# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 6

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 6 Science

### Grade 6 Science, Week 1

- **g6-science-w1-1: Changes of State**
  Competency: Describe changes of state for solids, liquids, and gases as melting, evaporation, freezing, condensation using diagrams and flowcharts.
  Starter question: What happens when water melts, evaporates, or freezes?
- **g6-science-w1-2: Heat and Changes of State**
  Competency: Explain the role of heat energy in change of state processes.
  Starter question: How does heat make matter change?
### Grade 6 Science, Week 3

- **g6-science-w3-1: Physical and Chemical Changes**
  Competency: Explain why physical changes are reversible, and chemical changes are irreversible.
  Starter question: Why can melted ice turn back but burnt paper cannot?
### Grade 6 Science, Week 4

- **g6-science-w4-1: Mixtures**
  Competency: Describe useful everyday examples of uniform and non-uniform mixtures, such as solutions and suspensions.
  Starter question: What is a mixture?
- **g6-science-w4-2: What Is Air Made Of?**
  Competency: Describe air as a mixture of oxygen, carbon dioxide, nitrogen, and water vapor.
  Starter question: What gases make up the air we breathe?
- **g6-science-w4-3: Separating Mixtures**
  Competency: Demonstrate various techniques in separating mixtures, such as decantation, winnowing, scooping, picking, evaporation, filtering, sieving, and using magnets.
  Starter question: How can we separate rice from stones or sand from water?
- **g6-science-w4-4: Why Separate Mixtures**
  Competency: Explain the benefits of each mixture separation technique in preparing useful products.
  Starter question: How does separating mixtures help us make things?
### Grade 6 Science, Week 7

- **g6-science-w7-1: Fair Test**
  Competency: Apply the features of a fair test: a. change one factor, b. measure one factor, and c. keep all other factors the same.
  Starter question: What makes an experiment a fair test?
- **g6-science-w7-2: Repeating Experiments**
  Competency: Recognize the features of a fair test and that scientific investigations also involve a) doing at least three trials, or use replication, and b) observing, measuring, and recording accurately.
  Starter question: Why do scientists repeat experiments?
### Grade 6 Science, Week 9

- **g6-science-w9-1: Circulatory System**
  Competency: Identify from pictures and diagrams the parts of the circulatory system as heart, blood, and blood vessels, and describe how they work.
  Starter question: How does my heart pump blood around my body?
### Grade 6 Science, Week 10

- **g6-science-w10-1: How Plants Reproduce**
  Competency: Describe the different ways that plants reproduce, such as pollination, seed production, and plant propagation.
  Starter question: How do plants make new plants?
- **g6-science-w10-2: Planning a Fair Test**
  Competency: Plan a simple scientific investigation that includes the features of a fair test, replication, and accurate measurement to determine which type of plant propagation, such as cutting, budding, layering, grafting, works best for garden plants.
  Starter question: How do I plan a fair experiment about plants?
