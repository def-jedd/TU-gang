# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 7

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies)

### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 1

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w1-1: Geography and Early Civilizations**
  Competency: Naipaliliwanag ang mahalagang ginampanan ng katangiang pisikal ng Pilipinas at ng rehiyon sa pagbuo ng sinaunang kasaysayan at kalinangan ng mga mamamayan sa Pilipinas at Timog-Silangang Asya
  Starter question: How did the land of Southeast Asia shape early civilizations?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 2

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w2-1: Peoples of Southeast Asia**
  Competency: Nasusuri ang heograpiyang pantao ng Timog-Silangang Asya batay sa pangkat-etnolinggwistiko, pananampalataya, estrukturang panlipunan, at ugnayang pangkapangyarihan
  Starter question: What groups of people live in Southeast Asia?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 3

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w3-1: Early Families and Society**
  Competency: Naiuugnay ang katangian ng sinaunang lipunan sa pagkakamag-anak, pamilya at kasarian (kinship, family and gender) sa Timog-Silangang Asya
  Starter question: How were families organized in early Southeast Asian societies?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 4

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w4-1: Austronesian Culture**
  Competency: Nasusuri ang kalinangang Austronesyano at Imperyong Maritima kaugnay sa pagbuo ng kalinangan ng Pilipinas at Timog-Silangang Asya
  Starter question: Who were the Austronesians and how did they shape our culture?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 5

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w5-1: Early Ties With China and India**
  Competency: Naiuugnay ang sinaunang kabihasnan ng Pilipinas sa mga bansa sa Timog-Silangang Asya, China, at India
  Starter question: How were early Filipinos connected to China and India?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 6

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w6-1: Geography and History**
  Competency: Napahahalagahan ang ugnayan ng heograpiya at sinaunang kasaysayan ng mga bansa sa Timog-Silangang Asya.
  Starter question: Why does geography matter in the history of Southeast Asia?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 7

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w7-1: Colonialism and Imperialism**
  Competency: Naipaliliwanag ang konsepto ng kolonyalismo at imperyalismo
  Starter question: What are colonialism and imperialism?
### Grade 7 Araling Panlipunan (Social Studies), Week 9

- **g7-araling_panlipunan-w9-1: Waves of Western Imperialism**
  Competency: Naipaghahambing ang una at ikalawang yugto ng imperyalismong Kanluranin
  Starter question: How were the first and second waves of Western imperialism different?

## Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g7-gmrc_values-w1-1: Judging Wisely**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa maingat na paghuhusga sa pamamagitan ng pangingilatis sa katotohanan at kabutihan na nakapaloob sa isang situwasyon a. Natutukoy ang mga katangian, gamit at tunguhin ng isip at kilos-loob b. Naipaliliwanag na ang gamit ng isip at kilos-loob sa sariling pagpapasiya at pagkilos ay ang nagsisilbing gabay sa pagpili at pagkilos na alisunod sa katotohanan at kabutihan, dahil ang mga ito ang nagpapabukod-tangi sa kaniya sa ibang nilalang c. Nailalapat ang wastong gamit ng isip at kilos-loob sa mga sariling pagpapasiya at pagkilos alinsunod sa katotohanan at kabutihan
  Starter question: How do I judge what is true and good in a situation?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 2

- **g7-gmrc_values-w2-1: Respect Through Helping**
  Competency: Naisasabuhay ang pagiging magalang sa pamamagitan ng pakikibahagi sa mga gawaing magpapabuti sa sarili, pamilya, at kapuwa a. Nakakikilala na ang dignidad ay ang batayan ng paggalang sa sarili, pamilya, at kapuwa b. Naipaliliwanag na ang dignidad ng tao bilang batayan ng paggalang sa sarili, pamilya, at kapuwa ay ang nagpapantay-pantay sa lahat ng tao dahil sa taglay niyang isip at kilos-loob, at ito ang nagbubunsod sa kaniya na gumawa ng mabuti c. Nakapaglalapat ng mga sariling kilos ng pagkilala sa dignidad ng sarili, pamilya, at kapuwa
  Starter question: How can helping others show respect?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 3

- **g7-gmrc_values-w3-1: Being Resilient**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging matatag sa pamamagitan ng palagiang paninindigan sa mga taglay na pagpapahalaga at virtue a. Nakakikilala ng mga paraan ng paggamit ng pagpapahalaga at virtue bilang batayan ng sariling pagpapasiya, pagkilos, at pakikipagkapuwa b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pagpapahalaga at virtue bilang batayan ng sariling pagpapasiya, pagkilos, at pakikipagkapuwa ay gabay na magtitiyak na patungo sa katotohanan at kabutihan ang bawat pagtugon lalo na sa mga situwasyon na sinusubok ang kanilang pagkatao c. Nailalapat nang wasto ang pagpapahalaga at virtue sa mga gagawing pagpapasiya, pagkilos, at pakikipagkapuwa
  Starter question: How can I stand firm on my values?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 4

- **g7-gmrc_values-w4-1: Faith in Hard Times**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pananalig sa Diyos sa pamamagitan ng pagbabahagi ng positibong pananaw sa pagharap sa mga hamon sa buhay a. Natutukoy ang mahalagang papel ng sariling pananampalataya sa buhay b. Naipaliliwanag na ang sariling pananampalataya sa Diyos ay nakatutulong sa pagkakaroon ng pag-asa, katatagan, at lakas ng loob (courage) sa pagharap sa mga hamon sa buhay c. Nailalapat ang sariling pananampalataya sa Diyos sa lahat ng oras lalo na sa mga mapanghamong situwasyon (hal. positibong pananaw sa kabila ng kahirapan)
  Starter question: How can faith help me face problems?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 5

- **g7-gmrc_values-w5-1: Good Stewardship**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging mabuting katiwala sa pamamagitan ng pagsisinop ng lahat ng bagay upang mapakinabangan hindi lamang ng sarili kundi ng kapuwa at pamayanan a. Nakapag-uugnay sa kahalagahan ng pagtitipid at pag- iimpok sa sariling pangangasiwa sa mga biyaya ng Diyos b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pagtitipid at pag-iimpok bilang sariling pangangasiwa sa mga biyaya ng Diyos ay pagiging mabuting katiwala ng mga kaloob Niya na magagamit sa pagtulong sa kapuwa at pamayanan c. Naisasakilos ang pagtitipid at pag-iimpok upang tulungan ang kapuwa at pamayanan ayon sa sariling kakayahan
  Starter question: How can I take good care of the things I have?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 6

- **g7-gmrc_values-w6-1: Being Prepared**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa kahandaan sa pamamagitan ng pagkakaroon ng emergency kit o katumbas nito batay sa sariling kakayahan a. Nakakikilala ng mga wastong pagtugon sa panahon ng kalamidad b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pansariling pagtugon sa panahon ng kalamidad ay paraan upang mailigtas ang buhay, malinang ang kahandaan sa pagharap sa mga panganib, mabawasan ang posibleng pagdurusa ng tao at makatulong sa kaligtasan ng kapuwa alinsunod sa mga alituntunin ng awtoridad c. Nailalapat ang mga pansariling pagtugon sa panahon ng kalamidad
  Starter question: What should be in an emergency kit?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 7

- **g7-gmrc_values-w7-1: Doing Our Part**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagigng mapanagutan sa pamamagitan ng panghihikayat sa iba na gampanan ang kanilang mga tungkulin bilang mamamayan a. Nakapagpapahayag ng mga paraan sa pagtupad ng sariling tungkulin bilang mamamayan b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pagtupad ng sariling tungkulin bilang mamamayan ay pakikibahagi niya sa pagpapabuti at pagpapatatag ng bayan para sa kapakinabangan ng mga mamamayan c. Nailalapat ang mga paraan ng pagtupad ng sariling tungkulin bilang mamamayan tulad ng paggalang sa mga karapatan ng kapuwa, pagsunod sa mga batas, pagiging mabuting pinuno, at tagasunod
  Starter question: How can I encourage others to do their duties?
### Grade 7 GMRC / Values Education, Week 8

- **g7-gmrc_values-w8-1: Asking for Advice**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa maingat na paghusga sa pamamagitan ng palagiang pagsangguni sa mga magulang o tagapangalaga tungkol sa mga karanasan kaugnay ng mga natutuhang pagpapahalaga a. Natutukoy ang mga pagpapahalagang natutuhan sa pamilya na may impluwensiya sa kaniyang pagkatao b. Naipaliliwanag na ang pamilya bilang sandigan ng mga pagpapahalaga ay may gampanin na hubugin ang mga anak sa mga pagpapahalaga c. Nailalapat ang mga natutuhang pagpapahalaga sa mga situwasyong kinakaharap
  Starter question: Why should I ask my parents before deciding?

## Grade 7 Mathematics

### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 1

- **g7-mathematics-w1-1: Regular and Irregular Polygons**
  Competency: Draw and describe regular and irregular polygons with 5, 6, 8, or 10 sides, based on measurements of sides and angles, using a ruler and protractor.
  Starter question: What is the difference between regular and irregular polygons?
- **g7-mathematics-w1-2: Drawing Polygons**
  Competency: Draw triangles, quadrilaterals, and regular polygons (5, 6, 8, or 10 sides) with given angle measures.
  Starter question: How do I draw a shape with given angles?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 2

- **g7-mathematics-w2-1: Angle Pairs**
  Competency: Describe and explain the relationships between angle pairs based on their measures.
  Starter question: What are angle pairs like complementary and supplementary angles?
- **g7-mathematics-w2-2: Classifying Polygons**
  Competency: Classify polygons according to the number of sides, whether they are regular or irregular, and whether they are convex or non-convex.
  Starter question: How do we group polygons?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g7-mathematics-w3-1: Exterior Angles**
  Competency: Deduce the relationship between the exterior angle and adjacent interior angle of a polygon.
  Starter question: How are the outside and inside angles of a polygon related?
- **g7-mathematics-w3-2: Angles of Polygons**
  Competency: Determine the measures of angles and the number of sides of polygons.
  Starter question: How do I find the angles of a polygon?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 4

- **g7-mathematics-w4-1: Percent Increase and Decrease**
  Competency: Solve problems involving:
  a. percentage increase
  b. percentage decrease
  Starter question: How do I find how much something went up or down in percent?
- **g7-mathematics-w4-2: Discounts and Interest**
  Competency: Solve money problems involving percentages (e. g., discount, commission, sales tax, simple interest).
  Starter question: How do I compute a discount or interest?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 5

- **g7-mathematics-w5-1: Making a Budget**
  Competency: Create a financial plan.
  Starter question: How do I make a plan for my money?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g7-mathematics-w6-1: Rates**
  Competency: Identify and explain the uses of rates.
  Starter question: What is a rate, like kilometers per hour?
- **g7-mathematics-w6-2: Rate Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving rates (e.g., speed).
  Starter question: How do I solve problems about speed?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 7

- **g7-mathematics-w7-1: Rational Numbers**
  Competency: Describe given rational numbers as fractions, decimals, or percentages.
  Starter question: How can one number be a fraction, a decimal, and a percent?
- **g7-mathematics-w7-2: Rational Numbers on a Number Line**
  Competency: Order rational numbers on a number line.
  Starter question: How do I order fractions and decimals on a number line?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 8

- **g7-mathematics-w8-1: Operations With Rational Numbers**
  Competency: Perform operations on rational numbers.
  Starter question: How do I add and multiply fractions and negative numbers?
### Grade 7 Mathematics, Week 10

- **g7-mathematics-w10-1: Square and Cube Roots**
  Competency: Determine the square roots of perfect squares and the cube roots of perfect cubes.
  Starter question: What is a square root?
- **g7-mathematics-w10-2: Irrational Numbers**
  Competency: Identify irrational numbers involving square roots and cube roots, and their locations on the number line.
  Starter question: What is an irrational number?

## Grade 7 Science

### Grade 7 Science, Week 1

- **g7-science-w1-1: Scientific Models**
  Competency: Recognize that scientists use models to explain phenomena that cannot be easily seen or detected;
  Starter question: Why do scientists use models?
- **g7-science-w1-2: Particle Model of Matter**
  Competency: Describe the Particle Model of Matter as “All matter is made up of tiny particles with each pure substance having its own kind of particles.”
  Starter question: What is matter made of?
- **g7-science-w1-3: Moving Particles**
  Competency: Describe that particles are constantly in motion, have spaces between them, attract each other, and move faster as the temperature increases (or with the addition of heat).
  Starter question: How do the tiny particles in matter move?
- **g7-science-w1-4: Particles in Solids, Liquids, and Gases**
  Competency: Use diagrams and illustrations to describe the arrangement, spacing, and relative motion of the particles in each of the three states (phases) of matter.
  Starter question: How are particles arranged in solids, liquids, and gases?
- **g7-science-w1-5: Changes of State and Particles**
  Competency: Explain the changes of state in terms of particle arrangement and energy changes:
  a. solid → liquid → vapor
  b. vapor → liquid → solid
  Starter question: What happens to particles when ice melts?
### Grade 7 Science, Week 4

- **g7-science-w4-1: Scientific Investigation**
  Competency: Follow appropriate steps of a scientific investigation which includes:
  a. Aim or problem
  b. Materials and equipment
  c. Method or procedures
  d. Results including data
  e. Conclusion.
  Starter question: What are the steps of a scientific investigation?
- **g7-science-w4-2: Accurate Measurements**
  Competency: Make accurate measurements using standard units for physical quantities and organize the collected data when carrying out a scientific investigation.
  Starter question: How do I measure accurately and record data in a table?
### Grade 7 Science, Week 6

- **g7-science-w6-1: Solute and Solvent**
  Competency: Identify the role of the solute and solvent in a solution.
  Starter question: What are a solute and a solvent?
- **g7-science-w6-2: Concentration**
  Competency: Express quantitatively the amount of solute present in a given volume of solvent.
  Starter question: How do we measure how much solute is in a solution?
- **g7-science-w6-3: Solubility**
  Competency: Demonstrate how different factors affect the solubility of a solute in a given solvent, such as heat;
  Starter question: Why does sugar dissolve faster in hot water?
- **g7-science-w6-4: Acids and Bases**
  Competency: Identify solutions, which can be found at home and in school and that react with a litmus indicator, as acids, bases, and salts.
  Starter question: How can litmus paper tell if a solution is an acid or a base?
- **g7-science-w6-5: Lab Safety**
  Competency: Demonstrate proper use and handling of science equipment.
  Starter question: How do I handle science equipment safely?
### Grade 7 Science, Week 8

- **g7-science-w8-1: The Microscope**
  Competency: Identify the parts and functions, and demonstrate proper handling and storing of a compound microscope.
  Starter question: What are the parts of a microscope and how do I use it?
### Grade 7 Science, Week 9

- **g7-science-w9-1: Looking at Cells**
  Competency: Use proper techniques in observing and identifying the parts of a cell with a microscope such as the cell membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria, and chloroplasts.
  Starter question: How do I see cells using a microscope?
- **g7-science-w9-2: One Cell or Many**
  Competency: Recognize that some organisms consist of a single cell (unicellular) like in bacteria and some consist of many cells (multicellular) like in a human.
  Starter question: What is the difference between unicellular and multicellular organisms?
- **g7-science-w9-3: Plant and Animal Cells**
  Competency: Differentiate plant and animal cells based on their organelles.
  Starter question: How are plant cells different from animal cells?
