# DepEd Term 1 lessons: Grade 8

Official competencies from DepEd's Three-Term Budgets of Work (BOW), copied word for word.
Each lesson has an id (g<grade>-<subject>-w<week>-<n>), a short title written by the TU-gang team,
the competency, and a child-friendly starter question. Teach ONLY what the competency asks.

## Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education

### Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education, Week 1

- **g8-gmrc_values-w1-1: Thinking About Emotions**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa maingat na paghusga sa pamamagitan ng pagninilay sa kamalayan sa mga emosyong nararamdaman, kilos, pag-iisip, at reaksiyon ng katawan. a. Naiisa-isa ang mga indikasyon ng pagkakaroon ng kamalayan sa mga emosyong nararamdaman. b. Napatutunayan na ang emosyong nararamdaman ay nakatutulong upang lubos na makilala ang sarili at makatugon nang wasto sa mga nararamdaman sa bawat situwasyon tungo sa pagpapaunlad ng sarili at ugnayan sa kapuwa. c. Naipakikita ang pagkilala sa mga emosyong nararamdaman.
  Starter question: How can understanding my emotions help me decide well?
### Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education, Week 2

- **g8-gmrc_values-w2-1: Positive Attitude**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging matatag sa pamamagitan ng mga gawaing nagpapakita ng pagpapahalaga sa mga positibong pananaw gabay ang pamilya. a. Nailalarawan ang positibong pananaw gabay ang pamilya. b. Naipaliliwanag na ang positibong pananaw gabay ang pamilya ay may impluwensiya sa kalusugang pangkaisipan at pangkatawan, na nagsisilbing kanlungan sa lahat ng panahon at ito ang naglilinang ng katatagan. c. Naisasakilos ang mga gawain na nagpapahalaga sa mga positibong pananaw gabay ang pamilya.

### Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education, Week 3

- **g8-gmrc_values-w3-1: Responding to Others' Needs**
  Competency: Nakapagsasanay sa pagiging mapagmalasakit sa pamamagitan ng pagiging sensitibo at wastong pagtugon sa pangangailangan ng kapuwa. a. Naipaliliwanag na ang pakikipagkapuwa-tao ay nakaugat sa kalikasan ng tao bilang panlipunang nilalang at naglilinang ng kaniyang kaganapan bilang tao sa pamamagitan ng paglilingkod sa kapuwa na indikasyon ng pagmamahal. c. Nailalapat ang mga paraan ng pakikipagkapuwa bilang isang kabataan.
  Starter question: How can I notice and help people in need?
### Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education, Week 4

- **g8-gmrc_values-w4-1: Avoiding Harmful Habits**

Naipaliliwanag na ang pananampalataya bilang gabay sa pag-iingat sa sarili ay pagpapanatili ng ugnayan sa Diyos at pagtiyak upang lubos na mapahalagahan ang buhay at makamit ang layunin ng kaniyang pagkalikha. c. Naisasakilos ang mga paraan ng pag-iingat sa sarili gabay ang pananampalataya.

### Grade 8 GMRC / Values Education, Week 5

Naiisa-isa ang mga sariling paraan ng pagdakila sa Diyos. b. ## Grade 8 Mathematics

### Grade 8 Mathematics, Week 1

- **g8-mathematics-w1-1: Mean, Median, and Mode**
  Competency: Determine measures of central tendency of ungrouped data.
  Starter question: What are the mean, median, and mode?
- **g8-mathematics-w1-2: Using Averages**
  Competency: Draw conclusions from statistical data using the measures of central tendency.
  Starter question: What can averages tell us about data?
- **g8-mathematics-w1-3: Algebraic Expressions**
  Competency: Model real-life situations using algebraic expressions.
  Starter question: How can I write a real-life situation using letters and numbers?
### Grade 8 Mathematics, Week 2

- **g8-mathematics-w2-1: Adding Monomials**
  Competency: Add and subtract simple monomials.
  Starter question: How do I add and subtract terms like 3x and 5x?
- **g8-mathematics-w2-2: Laws of Exponents**
  Competency: Multiply and divide simple monomials, leading to the derivation of the laws of exponents.
  Starter question: What are the laws of exponents?
### Grade 8 Mathematics, Week 3

- **g8-mathematics-w3-1: Multiplying Polynomials**
  Competency: Multiply simple monomials and binomials with simple binomials and multinomials, using the distributive property with various techniques and models.
  Starter question: How do I multiply expressions like (x + 2)(x + 3)?
- **g8-mathematics-w3-2: Special Products**
  Competency: Use special product patterns to multiply binomials.
  Starter question: What are special product patterns in algebra?
- **g8-mathematics-w3-3: Factoring**
  Competency: Completely factor different types of polynomials (polynomials with common monomial factor; difference of two squares; quadratic trinomials, including perfect square trinomials).

### Grade 8 Mathematics, Week 6

- **g8-mathematics-w6-1: Factoring Problems**
  Competency: Solve problems involving special products and factors of polynomials.
  Starter question: How do I use factoring to solve problems?
- **g8-mathematics-w6-2: Simplifying Rational Expressions**
  Competency: Simplify rational algebraic expressions.
  Starter question: How do I simplify a fraction with variables?
- **g8-mathematics-w6-3: Operations on Rational Expressions**
  Competency: Perform operations on rational algebraic expressions.
  Starter question: How do I add or multiply fractions with variables?
- **g8-mathematics-w6-4: Rational Equations**
  Competency: Solve problems involving simple rational algebraic equations (using cross-multiplication).
  Starter question: How do I solve equations using cross-multiplication?
### Grade 8 Mathematics, Week 9

- **g8-mathematics-w9-1: Number Patterns**
  Competency: Formulate the rule for finding the next term in a sequence by looking for patterns.
  Starter question: How do I find the next number in a pattern?
- **g8-mathematics-w9-2: The Coordinate Plane**
  Competency: Illustrate and describe the Cartesian coordinate plane.
  Starter question: What is the Cartesian coordinate plane?

- **g8-mathematics-w10-1: Plotting Points**
  Competency: Plot points on the Cartesian coordinate plane and determine the coordinates of a point on the plane.

## Grade 8 Science

### Grade 8 Science, Week 1

- **g8-science-w1-1: Digestive System**
  Competency: Using a labeled diagram, trace how food travels through the digestive tract and explain how different digestive processes work, including mechanical processing, secretion, digestion, absorption, and elimination.
  Starter question: How does food travel through my body?
- **g8-science-w1-2: Body Systems Working Together**
  Competency: Use models, flow charts, diagrams, and simulations to explain how body systems work together, such as digestion and excretion.
  Starter question: How do my body systems work together?
- **g8-science-w1-3: Plant Transport System**
  Competency: Describe how plant organs (leaf, stem, roots) work together as the transport system.
  Starter question: How do leaves, stems, and roots work together?
### Grade 8 Science, Week 3

- **g8-science-w3-1: Heredity**
  Competency: Represent patterns of inheritance of a simple dominant/ recessive characteristic through generations of a family.
  Starter question: Why do children look like their parents?
- **g8-science-w3-2: Predicting Traits**
  Competency: Predict simple ratios of offspring genotypes and phenotypes in crosses involving dominant/recessive gene pairs.
  Starter question: How can we predict which traits children will get?
### Grade 8 Science, Week 4

- **g8-science-w4-1: Classifying Living Things**
  Competency: Describe the importance of the six-kingdom system and the three-domain system of classification of living things.
  Starter question: What are the six kingdoms of living things?
- **g8-science-w4-2: Humans as Mammals**
  Competency: Explain why humans are classified under Class Mammalia and the Order Primates.
  Starter question: Why are humans called mammals and primates?
### Grade 8 Science, Week 6

- **g8-science-w6-1: Cycles in Nature**
  Competency: Using flow charts and labeled diagrams explain the role of plants and animals in the cycles of nature, such as the carbon, oxygen, and water cycles.
  Starter question: How do plants and animals help the carbon and oxygen cycles?
- **g8-science-w6-2: Photosynthesis and Respiration**
  Competency: Describe the process of photosynthesis and respiration, and identify its raw materials needed and products.
  Starter question: How do plants make food from sunlight?
- **g8-science-w6-3: Where Photosynthesis Happens**
  Competency: Using information from secondary sources identify the different parts of the cell where photosynthesis and respiration occur.
  Starter question: Which parts of the cell do photosynthesis and respiration?
- **g8-science-w6-4: Testing Photosynthesis**
  Competency: Plan a scientific investigation to verify the raw materials needed for photosynthesis.
  Starter question: How can I test what plants need to make food?
### Grade 8 Science, Week 10

- **g8-science-w10-1: History of the Atom**
  Competency: Develop a timeline for the historical background of the development of the current Atomic Model that identifies tiny particles as atoms.
  Starter question: How did scientists discover what atoms look like?
- **g8-science-w10-2: Structure of an Atom**
  Competency: Draw the structure of an atom in terms of the nucleus and electron shells.
  Starter question: What are the parts of an atom?
### Grade 8 Science, Week 11

- **g8-science-w11-1: Protons, Neutrons, and Electrons**
  Competency: Differentiate the subatomic particles protons, neutrons, and electrons in terms of their symbol, mass, charge, and location within an atom.
  Starter question: How are protons, neutrons, and electrons different?
